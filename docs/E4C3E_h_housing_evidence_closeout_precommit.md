# E4C3E — H housing evidence closeout

E4C3D identified one defensible numerical housing subcoordinate:

`H_ACCESS_SPACE_ROOMS_PER_PERSON = RMSP / NP`

It is dimensionless, higher-is-better, identical for OWNER and RENTER, and estimated from 2022 ACS housing records with the full housing weight and 80 SDR replicate weights.

E4C3E does not reinterpret outcome direction as an architecture gate. It only reports the frozen descriptive/inferential result under rules fixed before reading the comparison table in this closeout.

## Current operating representation

`H_SERVICE` remains valid CEX housing-service-flow evidence but is not promoted to a state coordinate.

`H_ACCESS_SPACE_ROOMS_PER_PERSON` is retained as the current numerical **space-access subcoordinate**.

This does not mean that space per person is a complete definition of Housing Economic Security and Access. Physical adequacy, housing insecurity, displacement/security, and other housing dimensions remain conceptually open.

The richer AHS adequacy/security route is preserved for future research and does not block progression of the current project.

## Cross-survey boundary

CEX H_SERVICE and ACS H_ACCESS are independent survey evidence. There is no person-level join, no estimated cross-survey covariance, and no automatic combined H scalar.

## Progression

A partial but identified H subcoordinate is scientifically preferable to manufacturing a full H scalar.

E4C3E therefore closes the current H workstream and authorizes E4C4 on employment/labor-security representation while keeping the broader H concept explicitly incomplete.

No five-component state vector, normalization, geometry, dimensionality test, Real Inflation estimate, or final scalar is authorized.
