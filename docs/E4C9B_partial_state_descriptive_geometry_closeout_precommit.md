# E4C9B — partial-state descriptive geometry closeout preflight

E4C9B is value-free. It reuses E4C9A only by exact artifact hashes and headers and opens zero distance, difference-vector, or contribution rows.

The purpose is to close the 2022 partial-state descriptive geometry cleanly before any narrative or next data expansion can retroactively change what the execution meant.

The complete 28-pair ledger remains the canonical descriptive record. The two named subsets frozen before geometry values were opened — owner/renter within age and adjacent age within tenure — may be reported as precommitted descriptive subsets. E4C9B does not authorize turning a post-hoc ranking of distances into a confirmatory result, nor selecting only the largest or smallest pairs after observing the table.

Both E4C7 metrics remain mandatory. The coordinate contribution ledger is an exact algebraic decomposition of squared metric distance; it is not a causal decomposition and the metric coefficients are not welfare weights.

Cross-sectional 2022 distance is not time change, an inflation rate, welfare loss, or an owner/renter inflation gap. Inferential distance statements remain blocked by missing cross-cell sampling covariance. Intrinsic dimensionality remains blocked by the lack of a precommitted noise-aware identification strategy.

The primary next branch is E4D0: a value-free multiyear partial-state comparability reconnaissance. It must determine which additional survey years can support the same semantics, cohort mapping, transforms, and survey-design lineage before any additional-year coordinate values are opened.
