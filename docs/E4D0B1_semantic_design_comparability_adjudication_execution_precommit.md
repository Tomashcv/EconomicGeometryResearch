# E4D0B1 — semantic + design comparability adjudication execution

E4D0B1 is the first phase allowed to open the semantic content of the 25 hash-pinned official E4D0A documents and the nine frozen static 2022 semantic authorities.

No microdata, economic result row, 2019 coordinate value, geometry, dimensionality calculation, or real-inflation estimate is opened or computed.

The adjudication universe is frozen to 40 objects obtained by expanding the 13 E4D0B axes across their relevant scopes: five coordinates, three survey families, and global policies.

The decision standard is deliberately conservative. Missing evidence in the current bundle is UNRESOLVED, not FAIL. A textual or coding difference is also UNRESOLVED unless official evidence explicitly demonstrates that the frozen estimand cannot be preserved. FAIL therefore requires affirmative incompatibility evidence.

Several objects are structurally decidable without inspecting economic values. The 2019 and 2022 official-release evidence can establish year availability; frozen transform formulas can be reused without refitting; missing years remain uninterpolated; and all source vintages are hash-pinned.

The K price-level bridge is intentionally not selected in E4D0B1. PRICE_LEVEL_OR_NOMINAL_DEFLATION_POLICY is therefore precommitted to remain UNRESOLVED until a separate pre-value bridge phase freezes the deflator/reference-scale transport rule. This prevents the 2019 K observations from influencing the choice.

For source-variable continuity, the executor uses the frozen 2022 implementation as the authority for which raw-variable candidates actually matter, then checks those identifiers and nearby official definitions in the 2019 and 2022 documentation. Candidate pools are not new economic hypotheses; they are a bounded parser vocabulary for recovering already-frozen implementation lineage.

If any object is UNRESOLVED, E4D1 remains blocked and the next route is targeted evidence/bridge resolution. If an explicit incompatibility yields FAIL, 2019 is rejected for the current temporal panel rather than repaired from observed outcomes.
