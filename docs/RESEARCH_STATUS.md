# Research status

## Established public facts

The project has frozen several useful descriptive results while explicitly limiting their interpretation.

### Macro coordinates

The early U.S. core experiment found that consumption purchasing power and housing access can move in opposite directions. A precommitted robustness attack did not fully survive, so the project does not promote that observation into a proof of multidimensionality.

A later precommitted change-point stage selected 2008 and 2013 under the primary BIC specification for the annual two-coordinate change vector. These are descriptive statistical segments only.

### 2022 partial state

The 2022 cross-sectional stage froze a partial state with:

- 8 state points;
- 5 numerical coordinates representing 4 concepts;
- 28 unordered pairwise difference vectors;
- two precommitted squared-distance metrics.

The stage explicitly did not authorize inferential geometry, PCA, affine-rank testing, causal coordinate attribution, welfare-loss interpretation or a Real Inflation scalar.

### Multiyear work

The next research family validates whether the 2022 estimator/coordinate machinery can be transported to earlier years without silently changing definitions. The public snapshot includes 2019 source-lineage, adapter and execution work, but full temporal geometry remains blocked until the required coordinate families are empirically validated.

## Not claimed

This repository does not currently claim:

- a final inflation scalar;
- an alternative official CPI/PCE replacement;
- a causal welfare measure;
- crisis prediction;
- machine-learning forecasting;
- a deployable financial strategy.
