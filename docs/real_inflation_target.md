# Research Target — Real Inflation / Economic Purchasing-Power Erosion

## Long-run objective

The ultimate research objective is to estimate the change in real economic
purchasing power experienced by a specific household or cohort.

This is broader than CPI or PCE inflation.

CPI and PCE remain valid benchmarks for consumer-price inflation. They are not
treated as false or manipulated measures.

The research construct will attempt to incorporate dimensions such as:

- consumption purchasing power;
- housing access;
- capital access;
- debt burden;
- income security;
- later, potentially durability/service-life and low-end access erosion.

For household/cohort h, let economic power be represented by:

    EP_h(t)

The eventual real economic purchasing-power erosion rate is conceptually:

    pi_real_h(t) = - Delta ln(EP_h(t))

Interpretation:

    pi_real_h > 0  -> economic purchasing power deteriorated
    pi_real_h = 0  -> no change
    pi_real_h < 0  -> economic purchasing power improved

The operational definition of EP_h(t), including dimensions and weights,
is NOT yet frozen.

No scalar Real Inflation index may be published until its construction
survives component validation, household/cohort tests, robustness tests and
out-of-sample replication.

E2A/E2B are component experiments, not estimates of final Real Inflation.
